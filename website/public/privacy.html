1. The short version

  • We collect what Kiwimake Cloud needs to make your apps: your account, your chats, your app’s build files and what you pay.
  • Your app’s code is kept on your own GitHub, not by us.
  • Your messages and code go to the AI services that run the agent (DeepSeek’s models through OpenRouter, or Claude on the Own key plan) so it can do the work.
  • We don’t sell your data, show you ads, or use trackers on this website.
  • You can download your data or delete your account in the app, any time.

4. Why we use it

  • To run Kiwimake Cloud for you (making and building your apps, delivering them, showing your chats and balance). This is needed to carry out our agreement with you.
  • To bill you and keep records the law asks for (tax and accounting).
  • To keep it safe and fair: stopping abuse, one free allowance per person, finding and fixing problems. This is our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To tell you about your account: sign-in codes, receipts, problems with a payment, and important changes. We don’t send marketing email unless you ask for it.

We don’t sell your personal data, share it for advertising, or use your chats or code to train AI models.

The legal bases above (contract, legal duty, legitimate interest) follow GDPR. For California (CCPA/CPRA) add the “categories” and “no sale or sharing” statements in the form the law asks for.

6. The AI and your data

  • Kiwimake’s AI agent runs on DeepSeek’s models. We reach them through OpenRouter, and through TheRouter when OpenRouter is down or refuses a request. The router passes each request to a host that runs the model: DeepSeek’s own service, which processes and stores data in [CHECK: the People’s Republic of China], or another host. Each host’s own terms decide how long it keeps the data and whether it may use it to improve its models. Sending EU, UK or US-state residents’ data to China needs its own transfer basis and a clear notice (and may need consent). Read DeepSeek’s platform terms and privacy policy on training and storage location. OpenRouter lets you exclude hosts that train on or keep prompts, and pick hosts by country: decide those settings and describe them here. Check TheRouter’s data policy too.
  • On the Own key plan, the agent runs on Claude, made by Anthropic, with your own Anthropic key, under your agreement with Anthropic. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models. Check Anthropic’s current commercial terms and data retention for API use before launch, and quote them exactly.
  • Don’t put passwords, secrets or other people’s private data in a chat. Anything in the chat goes to the AI.

7. Where it is stored

Mostly in the United States (Convex in US East, Instabox, OpenRouter, Stripe, Resend, GitHub). The AI requests may also be run by a host in another country, including China (section 6). If you live elsewhere, your data is sent to those countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK addendum), and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a US company is certified under it, for those transfers.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, apps and chats: while your account exists. When you delete an app, its machine is deleted at once, and for 10 minutes you can undo it. After that its chat, pictures, pages and build files are deleted for good.
  • Build files: the files of each app’s 3 newest versions. Older ones are deleted; they can be built again from your code.
  • Cloud machines: a machine is paused 5 minutes after work stops, saved and deleted after an hour, and the saved copy is deleted after 30 days without use.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes.
  • Downloads you ask for (your data, your signing keys): the link works for 30 minutes, then the file is deleted.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps them as tax law requires, even after you delete your account. We keep no copy of our own once your account is deleted.
  • Signing keys: while the app or your account exists. Save them first (Account, Save signing keys) if you want to keep updating your apps later, from Kiwimake on a computer. When you delete an app or your account, its keys stay locked away for 30 more days (write to us if you change your mind), then they are deleted for good. Without its key, an app on your phone can’t get new versions.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in our own encrypted backups for up to 30 days, and in our database provider’s copies for up to 7 days.

9. Your choices and rights

  • In the app: delete an app (and undo it within 10 minutes), remove your Own key, disconnect GitHub (or remove the Kiwimake App on GitHub), unlink a computer.
  • Your data, in the app under Account: Download my data gives you a copy of everything we hold about you in one file, Save signing keys saves your apps’ keys, and Delete my account cancels your plan and deletes your account and all its data at once, except the signing keys, which follow 30 days later.
  • By email to [PRIVACY EMAIL], from the address on your account, for anything else, such as a correction. We answer within one month.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may also have the right to object to some uses, to limit them, to take your data elsewhere, and to complain to your data protection authority. We don’t treat you differently for using these rights.
  • Deleting your Kiwimake account does not delete your repositories on GitHub: they are yours, and you delete them there.

10. Keeping it safe

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Signing keys, your Own key and GitHub tokens are encrypted again with keys of their own.
  • The AI key never sits on the machine where the agent works: it is added on the way out. The GitHub access a machine gets reaches one repository and expires within an hour. A signing key is on the machine only for the seconds it takes to sign, out of the agent’s reach.
  • If a breach puts your data at risk, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. This website and cookies

kiwimake.ai uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and has no forms. Cloudflare, which hosts it, sees your IP address to deliver the pages. When you pay, Stripe’s checkout page sets its own cookies to process the payment and stop fraud.

12. Kiwimake on your own computer

If you make apps on your own computer instead of the cloud, they are made there with your own AI sign-in, and we don’t receive your chats or code. Your computer and phone may download updates and the list of ready-made apps from kiwimake.ai and GitHub. If you choose to link that computer to a Kiwimake account (to move an app to the cloud, or to use the cloud when your computer is off), what you move is handled as this policy describes.

13. Children

Kiwimake Cloud is not for children under 16. If you think a child gave us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.
