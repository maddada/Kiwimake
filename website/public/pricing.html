CREDITS

What a credit gets you.

A credit covers everything a change needs: the AI doing the work, the machine it runs on, the build and the delivery to your phone. You never pay for storage.

A change like Usually uses
New words, colours or an icon About 1 credit
A new screen, or fixing a tricky part About 2 credits
A bigger feature across the app About 3 credits
A whole new app About 4 credits, but your first app never uses more than 3
How do I know what a change will use?

Before you send a message, Kiwimake shows an estimate under the message box, like “About 1 credit”. Bigger changes show a range on their plan, and the real number appears when the change is done. A change only starts when you have enough credits for the high end of its estimate.

Do unused credits roll over?

Plan credits roll over for one month while your plan is active, then expire. Free credits last until the end of the month. Extra credit lasts 12 months. We always use the credits that expire soonest first.

Who gets free credits?

Everyone with a Kiwimake Cloud account: 3 credits each month, given the first time you use the cloud that month. One free allowance per GitHub account.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, in the app: Account, Your plan, Manage plan. An upgrade starts right away: you pay the difference for the rest of the month and get the extra credits at once. A downgrade or a cancel takes effect at the end of the month you paid for (the year, on a yearly plan). Credits you already have stay until they expire.

What happens when I run out?

Kiwimake tells you before a change starts and offers extra credit or a plan. A change that is already running may go up to half a credit past your balance so it can finish; that comes out of your next credits.

Which AI does the work?

Kiwimake’s AI agent runs on DeepSeek’s models (V4 Pro, and V4.1 Flash for simple work), reached through OpenRouter, with TheRouter as a backup. On the Own key plan it runs on Claude, made by Anthropic, with your own key.

Is Kiwimake free on my own computer?

Yes. If you install the companion on your Windows, Mac or Linux computer, Kiwimake makes your apps there for free, with your own AI sign-in (Claude, Codex or Antigravity). Your AI provider’s usage or subscription costs may apply. You only need a plan for the cloud.
