Refunds and cancelling

How to stop a plan, when you get money or credits back, and what happens when a change goes wrong on our side.

Last updated [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Draft for review, not legal advice. Highlighted parts like [CONTACT EMAIL] are still to be filled in, and every “For review” note needs your answer or a lawyer’s eye before this page goes live.

The short version

  • Cancel any time. Your plan runs to the end of what you paid for.
  • If a change fails because of our machines or our build service, you get its credits back.
  • Changed your mind about a new plan? Full refund within 14 days if you used none of its credits.
  • Charges made by mistake are always refunded.

Cancelling a plan

  • Cancel in the Kiwimake app: Account, Your plan, Manage plan. That opens Stripe’s page for your plan, where cancelling takes two taps (you can also change your card and see receipts there). You can also write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account.
  • A cancel takes effect at the end of the month you paid for, or the end of the year on a yearly plan. Until then you keep your plan and its monthly credits.
  • Credits you already have stay until they expire. After that, you are on Free, with 3 free credits a month.
  • Deleting your account (Account, Delete my account) cancels your plan at once, along with everything else. If you want a refund under the 14-day rule below, ask before you delete it.
  • Moving to a smaller plan works the same way: it starts when the current month (or year) ends. Moving to a bigger plan starts at once.

Money back

  • Charged by mistake (a double charge, a charge after you cancelled, a wrong amount): we refund it in full. Write to us within 90 days.
  • New plans, 14 days: if you change your mind within 14 days of starting a new subscription and have used none of the credits it gave you, write to us and we refund it in full.
  • After that, and for extra credit: credits are not refunded as money. Consumers in the EU and UK have a 14-day right to withdraw from online purchases of digital content, extra credit packs included. It can only be taken away if, at checkout, they agree to start at once and confirm they lose the right (Stripe Checkout needs a consent tick box for that). Check whether extra credit should get the same 14-day rule as plans.
  • Yearly plans: a cancel stops the renewal, and the plan keeps working until the year ends. Unused months are not refunded. The 14-day rule above still applies to a new yearly plan.
  • If we close the cloud service, or close your account without a good reason under the Terms, we refund the unused part of what you paid.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days.

Credits back for a failed change

  • If a change fails because of our side (a cloud machine stopped, our build service failed, an outage), we give its credits back.
  • Not covered: a change that worked but you would like differently, or a build that failed because of the app’s own code after the agent’s three tries to fix it. In those cases, just ask the agent for the next change.
  • For now we do this by hand: write to [CONTACT EMAIL] within 30 days with the app’s name and roughly when it happened. Credits given back last 12 months, the same as extra credit.

The Own key plan

The $6 plan follows the rules above. What the AI costs on your own Anthropic key is billed by Anthropic, not by us, so we can’t refund it; ask Anthropic about those charges.

If a payment fails

If a renewal can’t be charged, Stripe tries again for a while. Your next monthly credits wait until it is paid, and the credits you already have stay. If it is never paid, the plan ends and you are on Free.

Before you dispute a charge

Kiwimake’s charges show on your card statement as SALEEM HADAD. Please write to us first: we answer within 2 business days and would rather fix it with you. While a dispute with your bank is open, we may pause the account’s paid features.
